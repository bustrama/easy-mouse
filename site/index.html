Three steps. One time.

After the first pairing, your phone finds the PC on its own.

  1. 1

    Open Easy Mouse

    Start the app on your PC. A small window shows a QR code.

  2. 2

    Scan with your phone

    Point the camera at the code. The remote opens in the browser.

  3. 3

    Allow once

    Confirm the phone on your PC. It is remembered from now on.

More than a touchpad.

  • Touchpad

    Move, click, scroll and drag with natural gestures, tuned for low delay.

  • Keyboard

    Type text in any language, Hebrew included, plus Ctrl, Alt, Win and shortcuts.

  • Media

    Play, pause, skip and volume for whatever is playing, with the title on screen.

  • Encrypted on your network

    Everything between the phone and the PC is encrypted and stays on your local network.

  • Reconnects itself

    Scan once. After that the phone finds your PC without a code.

  • Admin windows: not yet

    Administrator windows, the UAC prompt and the lock screen cannot be controlled in this version.

Stays on your network. Always.

A phone that can move your mouse needs real protection, even at home. Security is part of the design, not an add-on.

  • No cloud, no accountsThe phone talks straight to your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere else, apart from an optional once-a-day check for updates on GitHub.
  • You approve every deviceA new phone needs your click on the PC, with a matching code.
  • Encrypted and replay-proofTyped passwords are not readable by someone on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Revoke in one clickRemove a phone any time, or disconnect everything with a hotkey.
  • Open sourceRead every line. Releases are built in public and signed.

Ready in a minute.

Install, scan the QR, click Allow. That is the whole setup.
